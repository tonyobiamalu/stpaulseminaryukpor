ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Forming Minds, Shaping Hearts

St. Paul's Seminary, Ukpor provides quality secondary education alongside Catholic formation and the development of the Christian faith.

Our formation seeks to develop students intellectually, spiritually, morally and socially, preparing them for lives of meaningful service to God and humanity.
